This tool is for education only and is not tax, legal, or financial advice. Estimates use the 2027 IRS limits (Rev. Proc. 2026-24): $4,500 for self-only coverage, $9,000 for family coverage, and a $1,000 catch-up for age 55 and older. The IRS limit includes Hanna's contributions, so your maximum personal contribution is the limit minus Hanna's total contribution (the $500 upfront deposit plus the match). You must be enrolled in Hanna's High Deductible Health Plan to receive the deposit and to contribute, and you must contribute to receive the match. This tool assumes Hanna deposits $500 at the start of each year and then matches your contributions dollar for dollar until Hanna's total contribution reaches the amount selected ($900 for employee only or $1,200 for employee plus family). The investing calculator shows growth on the amount you enter at the annual return you choose, and optionally adds your yearly savings and Hanna's contributions in equal monthly amounts. Tax savings include federal, state, and 7.65% FICA on payroll contributions. Some states, such as California and New Jersey, do not give a state tax break on HSA contributions, so set the state rate to 0% if that applies to you. Actual investment returns will vary and are not guaranteed.
